# Privacy Policy

Last updated: **November 16, 2026**

## Introduction

This document is the Privacy Policy for the **Wane Academy** application (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), developed and operated by **Sirvan Bodagh** under the Wane Academy name.

Wane Academy is an educational platform designed to support the management of classes, educational activities, and learning progress. It provides separate features for Teachers and Students, including online classes, educational materials, assignments, examinations, academic records, and communication tools.

I respect your privacy and am committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, why it is needed, how it is used and protected, and what options are available to you.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information I Collect and Process

### 1.1. Account Information

Wane Academy does not currently offer public self-registration. User accounts are created and managed by the Academy for individuals who have previously registered for its educational services.

The following information may be associated with your account:

- **Full name**
- **Username**
- **Password or password-verification information**, used to authenticate your account
- **Email address** and other contact information, where provided
- **User role** (Teacher or Student)
- **Account status** (active, inactive, or suspended)
- **Assigned classes and group memberships**
- **Educational and administrative information** associated with your participation in the Academy

This information is used to identify users, manage their accounts, verify their identity, assign appropriate permissions, and provide access to the App's features.

### 1.2. Educational and Academic Information

As an educational management platform, Wane Academy processes information related to teaching and learning activities.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

- **Class schedules and session information**, including dates, times, and session status
- **Class participation and attendance records**
- **Assignments and educational activities**
- **Examination participation and submitted answers**
- **Examination results and academic reports**
- **Teacher comments and feedback** related to classes and educational activities
- **Learning materials and assigned electronic book content**
- **Educational progress and course-related records**

Teachers may use these features to manage classes, assign educational activities, activate examinations, review student performance, and provide feedback.

Students may use them to view their schedules, access learning materials, complete assignments and examinations, and review their academic results.

This information is processed to support the educational and administrative purposes of Wane Academy.

### 1.3. Messages and Communication Information

The App provides communication features that allow Teachers and Students to communicate within their assigned educational environment.

These features include:

- **Letters and internal messages** for announcements, notifications, and important educational information.
- **Group chat messages** for class coordination, sharing assignments, discussing educational activities, and communicating about class-related matters.
- **Message-related information**, such as sender, recipient, group, and message timestamps.
- **Attachments or other content** shared through communication features, where supported.

These communication features are provided for educational and administrative purposes, not as unrestricted private messaging services.

**Important:**

Communications within Wane Academy are managed within a controlled educational environment. Authorized Academy administrators may have access to relevant messages and communication records for educational coordination, moderation, security, investigating reported issues, and ensuring compliance with the Academy's rules.

Users should therefore not consider messages or group conversations within the App to be end-to-end encrypted or exclusively private communications between participants.

Access to these communications is intended to be limited to authorized individuals and legitimate operational purposes.

### 1.4. Online Classes and Recorded Sessions

Wane Academy supports online classroom sessions through an integrated web-based meeting environment.

When you participate in an online class, certain information may be processed by the meeting infrastructure, including:

- Your name and classroom identity
- Session participation information
- Audio and video streams, if you enable your microphone or camera
- Information necessary to establish and maintain your online meeting connection

Teachers may also upload recordings of class sessions for Students who need to review the lessons.

These recordings are made available to the relevant Students for a limited period of **one month** after they become available through the platform. After this period, access to the recordings is no longer provided through the App, subject to any necessary operational or legal retention requirements.

Recordings may contain the voices, images, names, or contributions of Teachers and Students who participated in the relevant session.

Users should be aware of this when participating in recorded classes.

### 1.5. Push Notification Information

The App uses push notifications to inform users about important educational and administrative updates.

Notifications may include:

- New letters or internal messages
- Class schedule changes
- Examination announcements or updates
- Important educational notices
- Other relevant account or class-related information

To deliver these notifications, the App may process a **device push token** and the **device type or platform** (for example, Android or iOS). This information helps determine the appropriate notification delivery route for your device.

Push notifications are delivered through notification infrastructure supported by your device's operating system and the services used by the App.

You can manage or disable notification permissions through your device's operating system settings. Disabling notifications may prevent you from receiving important updates promptly.

### 1.6. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the App's features and maintain your experience, certain information may be stored locally on your device, including:

- **Authentication information**, such as access tokens used to maintain your session
- **Cached account information**, such as your name and user role
- **Selected language preference** (English, Persian, or Kurdish)
- **Selected appearance theme** (Light or Dark)
- **Notification-related settings**
- **Temporary application data and cached content**
- Other technical information required for the App's normal operation

Locally stored information is managed by the App and the device's operating system.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored data, subject to the operating system's data-management behavior. However, uninstalling the App does not automatically delete your account or information stored on Wane Academy's servers.

### 1.7. Information Stored and Processed on the Server

The App communicates with Wane Academy's servers to authenticate users, retrieve educational information, manage classes, exchange messages, and provide other platform features.

Information stored or processed on the server may include:

- **Full name**
- **Username**
- **Password or password-verification information**, used to authenticate the account
- **Educational information**, including class assignments, schedules, attendance, activities, examination submissions, results, and other academic records
- **Account role and status**
- **Device type or platform** (for example, Android or iOS), used where necessary to identify the appropriate push notification delivery route
- **Device push token** or a similar technical identifier, used to deliver push notifications
- **IP address**, which may appear in standard server logs
- **Technical request information and timestamps**, where needed for security, troubleshooting, and operation of the platform

This information is used for account authentication, account and class management, educational record keeping, notification delivery, platform security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

The App stores the device type for notification and technical purposes; this does not mean that it necessarily collects the device's exact model. The App does not need the device model to identify whether a notification should be routed to an Android or iOS device.

Password information is used for authentication and is not intended to be displayed to other users. The security of stored password information depends on the authentication and storage mechanisms implemented by the service.

### 1.8. Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

The App's core features do not require access to your precise GPS location, contacts, or personal photo library.

The App does not intentionally collect such information for advertising or user profiling purposes. Certain features, such as camera or microphone access during online classes, are requested only when needed for those features.

## 2. Permissions I Request

Depending on the features you use and your device's operating system, the App may request the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose | Required? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Internet** | Authentication, accessing educational information, and communicating with Wane Academy servers | Yes |
| **Notifications** | Receiving letters, announcements, and important educational updates | No |
| **Microphone** | Participating in online classes when audio communication is required | Optional |
| **Camera** | Participating in online classes when video communication is required | Optional |

The availability of microphone and camera permissions depends on the online meeting features and the device's operating system.

I do not request access to your camera or microphone for advertising purposes.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Wane Academy may rely on third-party infrastructure and services to provide certain features of the App.

These may include:

- **Online meeting infrastructure:** Used to host and manage live classroom sessions.
- **Push notification services:** Used to deliver notifications to supported mobile devices.
- **Hosting and server infrastructure:** Used to store and process account information, educational records, messages, and other platform data.
- **App distribution platforms:** Google Play and Apple App Store, which may independently process information according to their own privacy policies.

These providers may process certain technical or operational information as necessary to deliver their services.

The App is not intended to provide advertising-based services, and personal information is not intentionally collected for targeted advertising through Wane Academy.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

## 4. Communication Monitoring and User Safety

Wane Academy provides internal communication features to support the educational relationship between Teachers and Students.

Since the App is intended for an organized educational environment, communication features are subject to Academy administration and management.

Authorized administrators may review relevant communications when necessary for:

- Monitoring appropriate use of the platform
- Managing educational activities and class-related communication
- Addressing reported inappropriate behavior
- Investigating security issues or violations of the Academy's rules
- Maintaining a safe and organized learning environment

Users must not use these communication features for harassment, inappropriate content, unauthorized advertising, or activities unrelated to the intended educational purposes of the platform.

## 5. Data Security

I take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Wane Academy.

These measures may include:

- **Secure communication:** Using HTTPS to protect information transmitted between the App and the server.
- **Authentication controls:** Restricting access to authorized accounts.
- **Role-based access:** Providing Teachers, Students, and administrators with different levels of access according to their responsibilities.
- **Server-side validation:** Managing access to educational records, class information, and other protected resources.
- **Administrative controls:** Limiting access to sensitive information to authorized personnel where applicable.

Access to information is intended to be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the operation of the platform and the provision of educational services.

Although I take reasonable steps to protect personal information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Wane Academy is an educational platform that may be used by children and teenagers, as well as adult learners.

For younger users, accounts are created and managed by the Academy rather than through unrestricted public registration.

The App is designed to provide educational services within an organized learning environment. Communication features are intended for interactions related to classes and educational activities, and these communications may be accessible to authorized Academy administrators as described in this Policy.

I aim to minimize the information processed and to handle children's information responsibly.

Where required by applicable law, the necessary parental or guardian consent and other safeguards must be obtained before processing a child's personal information.

The handling of children's information is subject to applicable data protection requirements, including relevant provisions of the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** in the United States and applicable children's data protection requirements under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** in the European Union.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following options regarding your personal information.

### 7.1. Request a Copy of Your Data

You may contact me to request information about the personal data associated with your account.

I will review your request and provide the information that can reasonably be accessed and disclosed, subject to applicable legal requirements and the rights of other users.

### 7.2. Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your Wane Academy account and its associated personal information, you may contact me using the email address provided in Section 10.

Since accounts are managed by the Academy, account deletion requests are handled through the Academy's administration rather than through public self-service registration or deletion.

I will review and process valid requests within a reasonable period, generally within 30 days where applicable.

Certain information may need to be retained for legitimate educational, administrative, security, or legal purposes, where permitted or required by applicable law.

Deleting your account may result in the loss of access to classes, educational materials, examination results, and other services associated with your account.

### 7.3. Manage Notifications

You can manage or disable push notifications through your device's operating system settings.

Please note that disabling notifications may prevent you from receiving timely information about new letters, class updates, examination announcements, and other important notices.

### 7.4. Remove Locally Stored Information

You can generally remove locally stored App data by uninstalling the App or using the relevant data-management features provided by your device's operating system.

Removing local data does not automatically delete your account or information stored on Wane Academy's servers.

## 8. Data Retention

Personal information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide educational services, manage accounts, maintain academic records, support platform operations, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Different types of information may have different retention periods.

In particular:

- Class recordings uploaded for Student access are available through the platform for **one month**.
- Account information may be retained while an account remains active and as necessary for account management.
- Academic records, examination results, attendance information, and educational history may be retained as necessary for educational and administrative purposes.
- Technical logs and security-related information may be retained according to operational and security requirements.
- Messages and communication records may be retained as necessary for educational coordination, administration, and legitimate security purposes.
- Device type and push notification identifiers may be retained while needed to deliver notifications and manage device registration.

Where information is no longer required, reasonable steps may be taken to delete it or remove identifying details, subject to applicable legal requirements and technical limitations.

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Wane Academy, its features, or its data-handling practices.

If significant changes are made:

- I will update the **Last updated** date at the top of this page.
- I may communicate important changes through a new App version, internal announcements, or another appropriate method.

I encourage users to review this page periodically.
